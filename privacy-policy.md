# Privacy Policy for Todo Reminder (iOS)

_Last updated: 2026-01-28_

This Privacy Policy explains how the Todo Reminder iOS app ("the App") handles your data.

The App is designed to work **entirely on your device**, without accounts, logins, or cloud syncing in the current version.

---

## 1. Data we store

### Task data and reminders
- The App stores your tasks, reminder times, and related settings **only on your device**.
- Data is saved using the device's local storage mechanism (AsyncStorage via Expo).
- We **do not** copy or send your task data to any server or third-party service.

### Notification settings
- The App uses local notifications to remind you about your tasks.
- Notification schedules (time, message, etc.) are also stored and processed **only on your device**.

---

## 2. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking

- The App currently has **no account system** and **no login**.
- We **do not**:
  - collect analytics or tracking information,
  - collect identifiers such as email address, phone number, or device ID,
  - use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

If a future version introduces accounts or server-based features, this Privacy Policy will be updated and you will be notified through an app update and/or updated store listing.

---

## 3. Permissions

### Notifications
- The App requests permission to send notifications in order to deliver local reminders at the times you choose.
- If you deny notification permission, you can still create and store tasks locally on the device, but you will not receive reminder alerts.

### Other permissions
- The App does **not** request access to your contacts, photos, camera, location, microphone, or other personal data in the current version.

---

## 4. Data retention and deletion

- All task data and notification schedules are stored on your device.
- If you delete a task inside the App, it is removed from the App's local storage.
- If you uninstall the App, all data stored by the App on your device is deleted by the operating system as part of the uninstall process.
- We do **not** keep server-side backups because no data is sent to our servers.

---

## 5. Children's privacy

- The App is not specifically directed at children.
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, as the App does not collect personal data or require an account.

---

## 6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add new features or change how the App works.  
When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes (such as introducing accounts or cloud sync), we will also update the app's store listing to reflect the new behavior.
